!! KEK LOSS = TOTAL DATA LOSS !!
The database and its backups contain ciphertext only. Losing
{{ .Values.kek.secretName }} loses every stored secret, irrecoverably. The
recovery chain is: the sealed form of the KEK in git, plus the sealed-secrets
controller's private key. Back up both, and never rotate the controller key
without re-sealing.

Passthrough payloads are a deliberate exception: their DEKs are wrapped under a
process-local ephemeral KEK that never leaves memory, so a restart loses live
passthrough payloads by design (clients get "payload lost — re-request"). That
is also why this Deployment is pinned to replicas: 1 with the Recreate
strategy, with no value to override it.
